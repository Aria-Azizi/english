Simple Present

Mastery of Facts, Processes, and Universal Truths

⏳ Timeline & Concept
Past NOW (General) Future

"It's not just about now; it's about ALWAYS."

⚙️ Morphosyntactic Structure
ACTIVE VOICE
(+) Subject + Base Verb (s/es)

"The engine works efficiently."

(-) Subject + do/does not + Base Verb

"The data does not support the theory."

(?) Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb?

"Does the policy improve life quality?"

PASSIVE VOICE (Band 8+)
(+) Subject + am/is/are + Past Participle

"The law is enforced globally."

(-) Subject + am/is/are not + Past Participle

"Safety is not compromised."

(?) Am/Is/Are + Subject + Past Participle?

"Is the waste recycled properly?"

Adverbs & Hedging
Frequency Adverbs: Always > Usually > Often > Seldom > Never

* Place before main verb, but after 'Be' verb.

Academic Hedging (Band 8):

Avoid 100% certainty. Use verbs like seem or appear.

"The trend appears to indicate a decline."
🛠️ Core Use Cases (When to use it)
1. Facts & General Truths

حقایق و واقعیت‌های همیشگی

To talk about things that are always true, scientific facts, or permanent situations.

"The Earth revolves around the Sun."
"He works as a software engineer."
2. Habits & Routines

عادت‌ها و کارهای روتین

To describe actions that happen regularly, often used with adverbs of frequency.

"I usually wake up at 6 AM."
"They go to the gym every weekend."
3. Scheduled Future Events

برنامه‌های زمان‌بندی شده در آینده

To talk about official timetables, flights, or public schedules (even if they are in the future).

"The train leaves at 8:30 tomorrow."
"When does the conference start?"
4. Narratives & Reviews

خلاصه‌ داستان و نقد فیلم/کتاب

Used dramatically to tell a story or describe the plot of a book or movie (Speaking Part 2).

"In the end, the hero saves the city."
"The author argues that..."
🎯 IELTS Strategic Application
Writing Task 1 Process Diagrams (Manufacturing & Nature)

Simple Present is mandatory for describing cycles. To get a Band 8, you must mix Active and Passive voices.

Band 8 Example: "Once the raw materials arrive (Active), they are sorted (Passive) by size."
Writing Task 2 Academic Truths & Scientific Reporting

Use it for citing research, universal claims, or stating your main thesis. Use "Hedging" verbs (suggest, indicate) instead of absolute words (prove).

Band 8 Example: "Evidence suggests that sedentary lifestyles contribute to obesity."
Grammatical Range Zero Conditional (Logical Certainty)

Used to show a 100% direct cause and effect. This structure proves to the examiner you can handle complex sentences.

If + Simple Present, Simple Present
Band 8 Example: "If the global temperature rises, polar ice melts at a faster rate."
🚫 Common Band 6 Pitfalls

Avoid these mistakes to secure a Band 7+ in Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA):

  • 1. Subject-Verb Agreement Failure: Forgetting the 3rd person 's' in complex subjects.
    ❌ The group of international students are...
    ✅ The group of international students is...
  • 2. The Stative Verb Trap: Using Continuous (-ing) for verbs of mind and possession.
    ❌ The new policy is consisting of three parts.
    ✅ The new policy consists of three parts.
  • 3. Confusion with Continuous: Using Simple Present for temporary actions happening right now.
    ❌ Currently, the government invests heavily in AI. (If it's a temporary ongoing trend)
    ✅ Currently, the government is investing heavily in AI.
