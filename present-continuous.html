Present Continuous

Mastery of Ongoing Actions, Evolving Trends, and Future Arrangements

⏳ Timeline & Concept
Past NOW (In Progress) Future

"It's not about ALWAYS; it's about RIGHT NOW or AROUND NOW."

⚙️ Morphosyntactic Structure
ACTIVE VOICE
(+) Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing

"The global population is rising rapidly."

(-) Subject + am/is/are not + Verb-ing

"The government is not doing enough."

(?) Am/Is/Are + Subject + Verb-ing?

"Are scientists developing a new cure?"

PASSIVE VOICE (Band 8+)
(+) Subject + am/is/are + being + Past Participle

"New policies are being implemented."

(-) Subject + am/is/are not + being + Past Participle

"The issue is not being addressed."

(?) Am/Is/Are + Subject + being + Past Participle?

"Is the data being analyzed correctly?"

Time Markers & Parallelism
Band 8 Time Markers: Currently | At present | Nowadays | Increasingly

* Use these instead of "now" for a more academic tone.

Parallel Structure (Band 8+):

Use while or as to show two ongoing trends happening at the same time.

"While the population is growing, resources are depleting."
🛠️ Core Use Cases (When to use it)
1. Actions Happening NOW

کارهایی که همین الان در جریانه

To talk about an action happening at the exact moment of speaking.

"Please be quiet, I am studying."
"It is raining outside right now."
2. Temporary Situations & Trends

شرایط موقت و روندهای در حال تغییر

To describe situations that are currently true but will likely change (not permanent).

"She is living with her parents until she finds a job."
"Housing prices are going up."
3. Fixed Future Arrangements

برنامه‌های قطعی در آینده شخصی

To talk about plans you have already organized and fixed for the future.

"I am meeting the manager tomorrow at 10 AM."
"We are flying to Paris next week."
4. Annoying Habits (with 'always')

عادت‌های آزاردهنده (شکایت کردن)

Used with 'always' or 'constantly' to express irritation about someone's repeated behavior.

"He is always complaining about his salary."
"They are constantly interrupting me."
🎯 IELTS Strategic Application
Writing Task 1 Describing Dynamic Trends (Graphs/Charts)

If the chart shows data that is currently happening or evolving right now, use Present Continuous to highlight the movement rather than just the fact.

Band 8 Example: "The number of active users is steadily rising, while traditional subscriptions are declining."
Writing Task 2 Discussing Societal Shifts & Evolving Problems

Use it in your introduction or body paragraphs to describe how the world is actively changing right now.

Band 8 Example: "Modern society is becoming increasingly dependent on automated technology."
Speaking Part 3 Evaluating Change (Past vs. Present)

Examiners often ask "How have things changed?". Contrast the past with what people are doing *nowadays* using this tense.

Band 8 Example: "In the past, people shopped locally, but nowadays, consumers are purchasing almost everything online."
🚫 Common Band 6 Pitfalls

Avoid these mistakes to secure a Band 7+ in Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA):

  • 1. The Stative Verb Trap (CRITICAL): Using -ing with verbs of emotion, possession, or mental states (like, want, need, know, belong, understand).
    ❌ I am understanding the main cause of this issue.
    ✅ I understand the main cause of this issue.
  • 2. Missing the "To Be" Verb: A very common slip under exam pressure. The -ing verb cannot stand alone.
    ❌ The global economy recovering slowly.
    ✅ The global economy is recovering slowly.
  • 3. Forgetting "being" in the Passive Voice: When transforming sentences to passive, students often accidentally create a Simple Present Passive instead of Continuous.
    ❌ A new hospital is built in our city. (Means it's already finished/a fact)
    ✅ A new hospital is being built in our city. (Means construction is happening right now)
