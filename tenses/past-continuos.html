Past Continuous

Mastery of Background Context, Interrupted Actions, and Parallel Events

⏳ Timeline & Concept
Past Action in Progress NOW Future

"It was happening in the background when suddenly, something else happened."

⚙️ Morphosyntactic Structure
ACTIVE VOICE
(+) Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

"The economy was recovering slowly before the crash."

(-) Subject + was/were not + Verb-ing

"Citizens were not paying attention to the warnings."

(?) Was/Were + Subject + Verb-ing?

"Were authorities monitoring the situation?"

PASSIVE VOICE (Band 8+)
(+) Subject + was/were + being + Past Participle

"New infrastructure was being developed across the region."

(-) Subject + was/were not + being + Past Participle

"The old methods were not being used anymore."

(?) Was/Were + Subject + being + Past Participle?

"Was the data being recorded properly?"

Advanced Mechanics: When vs. While
WHILE (Continuous Action):

Always followed by the Past Continuous.

"While the population was growing, resources declined." WHEN (Interruption):

Usually followed by the Simple Past.

"The system was operating fine when the power failed."
🛠️ Core Use Cases (When to use it)
1. Interrupted Actions in the Past

کارهای استمراری که قطع شدند

An ongoing action in the past was interrupted by a shorter action (Simple Past).

"I was sleeping when the phone rang."
"He was driving fast when the police stopped him."
2. Parallel Actions

دو کار استمراری همزمان در گذشته

When two continuous actions were happening at exactly the same time in the past.

"While she was studying, I was making dinner."
"The team was researching while the board was debating."
3. Setting the Scene (Storytelling)

فضاسازی در داستان و روایت

To describe the background and atmosphere at the beginning of a story.

"The sun was shining and the birds were singing."
"Everyone was talking loudly in the room."
4. Specific Time as an Interruption

استمرار در یک زمان دقیق در گذشته

Used when stating exactly what was happening at a specific clock time in the past.

"At 8 PM last night, I was watching a documentary."
"They were having lunch at noon."
🎯 IELTS Strategic Application
Speaking Part 2 Storytelling & Setting the Scene (GRA score)

To get a Band 7+ in Speaking, you must show you can manipulate time. Start your Cue Card story by setting the background with Past Continuous before jumping into the main events (Simple Past).

Band 8 Example: "I remember it vividly. I was preparing for my final exams and the pressure was mounting, when suddenly..."
Writing Task 2 Providing Historical Context (Introductions)

Use Past Continuous to explain a trend that was happening in the past to contrast it with what is happening today.

Band 8 Example: "In the late 20th century, societies were gradually transitioning towards digital communication; however, today..."
Writing Task 1 Parallel Trends in the Past (Less Common but Powerful)

If you have two lines on a graph in the past that cross each other, you can use "While" + Past Continuous to show the contrast.

Band 8 Example: "While the consumption of fossil fuels was rapidly increasing, renewable energy sources remained flat."
🚫 Common Band 6 Pitfalls

Avoid these mistakes to secure a Band 7+ in Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA):

  • 1. The "While vs. When" Confusion: Using "while" before a short, sudden action instead of a continuous one.
    ❌ While the phone rang, I was sleeping.
    ✅ When the phone rang, I was sleeping. OR While I was sleeping, the phone rang.
  • 2. Stative Verbs in the Past: The rule applies here too! Verbs of state (know, want, belong) cannot be continuous, even in the past.
    ❌ At that time, I was knowing the answer.
    ✅ At that time, I knew the answer.
  • 3. Forgetting "being" in the Past Passive: A severe error that changes the meaning entirely.
    ❌ In 2010, the new policy was discussed by the committee. (Means the discussion was finished).
    ✅ In 2010, the new policy was being discussed by the committee. (Means the discussion was ongoing at that time).
