Simple Past

Mastery of Finished Events, Historical Data, and Narratives

⏳ Timeline & Concept
Past Finished Action NOW Future

"It started in the past, and it ended in the past. 100% disconnected from NOW."

⚙️ Morphosyntactic Structure
ACTIVE VOICE
(+) Subject + Past Verb (V2)

"Sales plummeted in 2008."

(-) Subject + did not + Base Verb

"The company did not recover quickly."

(?) Did + Subject + Base Verb?

"Did the policy change consumer behavior?"

PASSIVE VOICE (Band 8+)
(+) Subject + was/were + Past Participle

"The new law was introduced in 1999."

(-) Subject + was/were not + Past Participle

"The targets were not met that year."

(?) Was/Were + Subject + Past Participle?

"Was the budget approved by the board?"

Irregular Verbs Alert:

Many academic verbs are irregular and don't take 'ed'. You must memorize them.

Fall → Fell | Rise → Rose | Undergo → Underwent
🛠️ Core Use Cases (When to use it)
1. Finished Actions

کارهای تمام شده در زمان مشخص

Actions that started and finished at a specific, stated time in the past.

"The company launched its product in 2015."
"I graduated from university three years ago."
2. Series of Completed Actions

روایت داستان و اتفاقات متوالی

To list actions that happened one after another in a chronological story.

"He arrived, checked into the hotel, and slept."
"First, the temperature dropped, then the machine stopped."
3. Past Habits & States

عادت‌ها یا شرایط در گذشته (که الان نیست)

Actions or facts that were true in the past but are not true anymore.

"She played the piano when she was a child."
"People wrote letters before the internet."
4. Hypothetical Situations

شرایط فرضی و غیرواقعی (شرطی نوع دوم)

Used after 'if' or 'wish' to talk about imagined situations in the present or future.

"If I had a million dollars, I would invest it."
"I wish I knew the answer."
🎯 IELTS Strategic Application
Writing Task 1 Reporting Historical Data (Line/Bar/Pie Charts)

If the chart's timeline is entirely in the past (e.g., 1990 to 2010), you must use Simple Past to describe the trends. Mix active and passive structures for Band 8.

Band 8 Example: "In 2005, the proportion of renewable energy surged (Active), while fossil fuels were gradually phased out (Passive)."
Speaking Part 2 Storytelling & Describing Experiences

When asked to "Describe a time when...", your primary tool is the Simple Past. A common mistake is accidentally switching back to Present tense mid-story.

Band 8 Example: "When I initially arrived in the city, I found it quite overwhelming."
Grammatical Range Second Conditional (Hypothetical Solutions)

In Task 2, use this to present a hypothetical solution and its result. This demonstrates advanced grammatical flexibility (GRA).

If + Simple Past, Subject + would + Base Verb
Band 8 Example: "If governments allocated more funding to education, poverty rates would decrease."
🚫 Common Band 6 Pitfalls

Avoid these mistakes to secure a Band 7+ in Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA):

  • 1. The Double Past Error (Negative/Question): Putting the main verb in the past tense when 'did' is already doing that job.
    ❌ The figures did not rose in 2010.
    ✅ The figures did not rise in 2010.
  • 2. Missing "was/were" in Passive Voice: Especially common in Task 1 when describing things that were built or created.
    ❌ The new library built in 1995. (This means the library physically built something itself!)
    ✅ The new library was built in 1995.
  • 3. Present Perfect vs. Simple Past Confusion: Using Present Perfect (has/have + V3) when a specific past time is mentioned.
    ❌ The economy has recovered last year.
    ✅ The economy recovered last year.
